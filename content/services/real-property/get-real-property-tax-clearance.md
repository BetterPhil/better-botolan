# Get a Real Property Assessment & Tax Clearance

How to secure a Real Property Clearance Certificate from the Assessor — commonly required when applying for a business permit.

> **Official Citizen's Charter.** The requirements, fees and processing times below are transcribed from the Municipal Assessor's Office Citizen's Charter of the Municipality of Botolan. The office's exact location and hours are **to be confirmed** — please call before you go.

---

## What to bring

- Fully Accomplished Application Form — _From the BPLO_
- DTI Certificate — _From the BPLO_
- Real Property Tax Receipt (latest year) — _Municipal Treasurer_
- Tax Declaration (1 photocopy) — _Municipal Treasurer_
- Barangay Clearance — _From the Punong Barangay_
- Barangay Zoning Clearance — _From the Punong Barangay_

## Step by step

1. **Interview and review of the presented documents.**
   - _You:_ Submit the requirements and wait for evaluation.
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

2. **The Tax Mapper checks the location of the property on the tax map.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 minutes · Handled by: Tax Mapper

3. **The clerk processes and records the transaction.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 10 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

4. **The Municipal Assessor approves and signs the Real Property Clearance Certificate for Business.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 10 minutes · Handled by: Municipal Assessor

**Total — Fee: None · Processing time: 30 minutes**
