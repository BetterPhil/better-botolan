# Transfer a Tax Declaration to a New Owner

How to update a property’s tax declaration after a sale, inheritance, or donation so it reflects the new owner.

> **Official Citizen's Charter.** The requirements, fees and processing times below are transcribed from the Municipal Assessor's Office Citizen's Charter of the Municipality of Botolan. The office's exact location and hours are **to be confirmed** — please call before you go.

_With PASSO (Provincial Assessor) approval._

---

## What to bring

- Deed of Conveyance (Sale, Inheritance, or Donation) (1 photocopy) — _From the owner_
- Certificate of Real Property Tax Payment (1 photocopy) — _Office of the Municipal Treasurer_
- Transfer Tax Receipt / Certification (1 photocopy) — _Office of the Provincial Treasurer_
- Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR) (1 photocopy) — _Bureau of Internal Revenue_
- Certified True Copy of Title (1 copy) — _Registry of Deeds_
- PARO Clearance if agricultural land (1 photocopy) — _Department of Agrarian Reform – PARO_
- Approved Subdivision Plan if segregated or consolidated (2 blueprint copies) — _From the owner_

## Step by step

1. **Interview and review of the presented documents.**
   - _You:_ Inform any of the staff about your request.
   - Fee: None · Time: 10 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

2. **The Assessor and staff, with you, conduct an ocular inspection to assess the value.**
   - _You:_ Submit the requirements and wait for evaluation.
   - Fee: None · Time: 2 hours · Handled by: Tax Mapper, Property Appraiser, Municipal Assessor

3. **Preparation of the Updated Tax Declaration.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 15 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

4. **Recording, processing, and approval at the PASSO, then receiving the approved Tax Declaration.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 working days · Handled by: Municipal Assessor

5. **Recording and issuance of the owner's copy from the Municipal Assessor's Office.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 15 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

**Total — Fee: None · Processing time: 5 working days, 2 hours, and 40 minutes**
