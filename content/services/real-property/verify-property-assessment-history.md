# Verify a Property's Assessment History

How to research the history of real property tax assessments or a tax declaration.

> **Official Citizen's Charter.** The requirements, fees and processing times below are transcribed from the Municipal Assessor's Office Citizen's Charter of the Municipality of Botolan. The office's exact location and hours are **to be confirmed** — please call before you go.

---

## What to bring

- Certified True Copy of Title (1 copy) — _From the owner_
- Related Reference Documents — _From the owner_

## Step by step

1. **The Assessment Clerk verifies and researches the history of the real property.**
   - _You:_ Inform any of the staff about your request.
   - Fee: None · Time: 40 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk, Tax Mapper

2. **The Assessment Clerk presents the history of the real property to you.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk, Tax Mapper

3. **Recording and issuance of a machine copy of the Tax Declaration from the Municipal Assessor's Office.**
   - Fee: ₱50.00 (photocopied documents) · Time: 15 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

**Total — Fee: ₱50.00 · Processing time: 1 hour**
