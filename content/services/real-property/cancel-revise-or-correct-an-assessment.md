# Cancel, Revise, or Correct a Property Assessment

How to fix or cancel an assessment — for example when a property no longer exists or its recorded details are wrong.

> **Official Citizen's Charter.** The requirements, fees and processing times below are transcribed from the Municipal Assessor's Office Citizen's Charter of the Municipality of Botolan. The office's exact location and hours are **to be confirmed** — please call before you go.

_With PASSO (Provincial Assessor) approval._

---

## What to bring

- Letter of Request for Cancellation, Revision, or Correction of Assessment — _From the owner_
- Certificate of Real Property Tax Payment (1 copy) — _Office of the Municipal Treasurer_
- Barangay Certification that the property no longer exists (1 copy) — _From the Punong Barangay_

## Step by step

1. **The Assessor, with you, conducts an ocular inspection to check for basis. A Field Appraisal and Assessment Sheet (FAAS) is prepared.**
   - _You:_ Inform any of the staff about your request.
   - Fee: None · Time: 2 hours · Handled by: Assessment Clerk, Property Appraiser, Municipal Assessor

2. **The Assessor prepares an inspection report, then a Notice of Cancellation, Revision, or Correction.**
   - _You:_ Submit the requirements and wait for evaluation.
   - Fee: None · Time: 1 hour · Handled by: Assessment Clerk, Property Appraiser, Municipal Assessor

3. **Recording, processing, and approval of the notice at the PASSO, then receiving the approved notice.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 working days · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

4. **Recording of the Notice of Cancellation, Revision, or Correction.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 10 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

5. **Issuance of the owner's copy from the Municipal Assessor's Office.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

**Total — Fee: None · Processing time: 5 working days, 3 hours, and 15 minutes**
