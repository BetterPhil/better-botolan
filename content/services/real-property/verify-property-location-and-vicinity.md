# Verify a Property's Location and Vicinity

How to confirm where a property sits on the tax map and get a copy of its location.

> **Official Citizen's Charter.** The requirements, fees and processing times below are transcribed from the Municipal Assessor's Office Citizen's Charter of the Municipality of Botolan. The office's exact location and hours are **to be confirmed** — please call before you go.

---

## What to bring

- One (1) photocopy of any of: Tax Declaration, Sketch Plan, or Land Title — _From the owner_

## Step by step

1. **The Tax Mapper verifies and researches the location of the property on the tax map.**
   - _You:_ Inform any of the staff about your request.
   - Fee: None · Time: 10 minutes · Handled by: Tax Mapper, Draftsman

2. **The Tax Mapper presents the vicinity of the property to you.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 minutes · Handled by: Tax Mapper

3. **Issuance of a copy of the Property Location from the Municipal Assessor's Office.**
   - Fee: ₱200.00 · Time: 15 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk, Tax Mapper

**Total — Fee: ₱200.00 · Processing time: 30 minutes**
