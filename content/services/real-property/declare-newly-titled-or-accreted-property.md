# Declare a Newly Titled or Accreted Property

How to get the first tax declaration for a newly titled residential or agricultural lot, or for land gained through accretion outside a cadastral lot.

> **Official Citizen's Charter.** The requirements, fees and processing times below are transcribed from the Municipal Assessor's Office Citizen's Charter of the Municipality of Botolan. The office's exact location and hours are **to be confirmed** — please call before you go.

_With PASSO (Provincial Assessor) approval._

---

## What to bring

- Certified True Copy of Original Certificate of Title (OCT) (1 copy) — _From the owner_
- (For accretion) Affidavit of Ownership (1 copy) — _From the owner_
- (For accretion) Affidavit of Two (2) Disinterested Persons Adjacent to the Lot (1 copy) — _From the owner_
- (For accretion) Certificate of Alienable and Disposable Land and Approved Sketch Plan (1 copy) — _DENR – CENRO_
- (For accretion) Certificate of Actual Occupancy for more than ten (10) years (1 copy) — _From the Punong Barangay_
- (For accretion) Certification of Lot Status — _DENR-CENRO, DAR-PARO, RTC, or LRA_

## Step by step

1. **The Assessor, with you, conducts an ocular inspection of the property. A Field Appraisal and Assessment Sheet (FAAS) is prepared.**
   - _You:_ Inform any of the staff about your request.
   - Fee: None · Time: 2 hours · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

2. **Preparation of the New Tax Declaration.**
   - _You:_ Submit the requirements and wait for evaluation.
   - Fee: None · Time: 1 hour · Handled by: Assessment Clerk, Tax Mapper, Property Appraiser, Municipal Assessor

3. **Recording, processing, and approval at the PASSO, then receiving the approved Tax Declaration.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 working days · Handled by: Municipal Assessor

4. **Recording and issuance of the owner's copy from the Municipal Assessor's Office.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 15 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

**Total — Fee: None · Processing time: 5 working days, 3 hours, and 15 minutes**
