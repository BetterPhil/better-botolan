# Request Certified Copies or Certifications

How to get a certified photocopy, a machine copy, or a certification of no/with improvement or no property.

> **Official Citizen's Charter.** The requirements, fees and processing times below are transcribed from the Municipal Assessor's Office Citizen's Charter of the Municipality of Botolan. The office's exact location and hours are **to be confirmed** — please call before you go.

---

## What to bring

- Verbal Request — _From the owner_
- Official Receipt of Payment — _From the owner_

## Step by step

1. **Evaluation of the request.**
   - _You:_ Inform any of the staff about your request.
   - Fee: ₱100.00 (certifications); ₱50.00 (machine copies) · Time: 5 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

2. **Preparation of the Tax Declaration or Certification.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 minutes · Handled by: Assessment Clerk

3. **The Municipal Assessor approves and signs the true copy or certification.**
   - Fee: None · Time: 5 minutes · Handled by: Municipal Assessor

**Total — Fee: ₱150.00 · Processing time: 15 minutes**
