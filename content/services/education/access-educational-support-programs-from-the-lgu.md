# Access Educational Support Programs

Where students and parents can find education assistance in Botolan.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- Students, parents and guardians in Botolan.

## How it works

1. **School feeding and supplies:** ask your principal about DepEd programs.
2. **Conditional cash grants:** 4Ps households get education support. Ask the MSWDO.
3. **Student jobs:** **SPES** gives students temporary work during breaks, through the **PESO**.
4. **Local support:** the Local School Board uses the **Special Education Fund** for local school needs.

## What to bring

- Student ID or school certificate
- Parent/guardian ID
- Proof of residency
- Certificate of indigency where required
