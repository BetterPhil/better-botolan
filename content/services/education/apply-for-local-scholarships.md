# Apply for Scholarships

National and local scholarship options for Botolan students.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- Students from Botolan in senior high, college or tech-voc training.

## How it works

1. **Check national programs first:** CHED and UniFAST assistance, and free tuition at state and local colleges.
2. **Ask the Mayor's Office and Sangguniang Bayan** about any local scholarship and its deadlines.
3. **Ask your school.** The **Polytechnic College of Botolan** and your senior high can explain options.
4. **Prepare documents early** and submit before the deadline.

## What to bring

- Application form
- Report card / school records
- Proof of residency
- Certificate of indigency or family income if required
- Birth certificate and valid ID

## Legal basis

- **Republic Act No. 10931** (Free Higher Education Act): free tuition in SUCs/LUCs and subsidies for poor students.

## Tips

- Deadlines are short. Check announcements monthly.
