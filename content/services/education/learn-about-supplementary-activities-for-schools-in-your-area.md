# Learn About Supplementary School Activities

Programs beyond regular classes for Botolan learners.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- Students, parents and teachers.

## How it works

1. **Ask your school** about contests, sports, clubs and training.
2. **Follow the DepEd Schools Division of Zambales** for division-wide events.
3. **Out-of-school youth and adults:** the **Alternative Learning System (ALS)** completes basic education outside the classroom.
4. **Parents:** join your school's PTA.

## What to bring

- Student ID or school record
- Parent/guardian consent for minors
