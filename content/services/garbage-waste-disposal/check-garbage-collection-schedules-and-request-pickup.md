# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup

How garbage collection works in Botolan.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- All households and businesses in Botolan.

## How it works

1. **Find your barangay's collection schedule:** {GARBAGE_SCHEDULE}
2. **Segregate your waste** before collection (see the segregation guide).
3. **Put garbage out on time** in a covered container at the pickup point.
4. **Missed pickup?** Report it to your barangay hall or the MENRO.

## What to bring

- Nothing required. Just have your waste properly segregated.

## Legal basis

- **Republic Act No. 9003** (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act).

## Tips

- Schedules can change. Check with your barangay.
