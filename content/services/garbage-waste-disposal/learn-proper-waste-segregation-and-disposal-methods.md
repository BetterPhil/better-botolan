# Learn Proper Waste Segregation and Disposal

How to separate waste at home so it can be collected and recycled properly.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- Every household, business and school.

## How it works

1. **Biodegradable:** food and garden waste. Compost if you can.
2. **Recyclable:** paper, cardboard, glass, metal, clean plastics. Keep them clean and dry.
3. **Residual:** what can't be recycled or composted goes in the regular garbage.
4. **Special waste:** batteries, lamps, paint, medical waste and electronics must not be mixed in.
5. **Never burn or dump waste.** Open burning and open dumping are prohibited.

## What to bring

- Separate containers for each type of waste
