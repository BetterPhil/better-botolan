# Report Illegal Dumping or Waste Violations

How to report dumping, burning and other waste violations.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- Anyone who sees illegal dumping, open burning or littering.

## How it works

1. **Note the details:** place, date, time, what you saw. Photo if safe.
2. **Report to your barangay hall first.**
3. **Escalate** to the MENRO or Municipal Hall if it continues.
4. **For serious cases,** report to DENR.

## What to bring

- Your notes and photos
- Your name and contact (you may ask to keep them confidential)

- **Republic Act No. 9003** prohibits littering, open burning and open dumping.

## Tips

- Do not confront violators. Report instead.
