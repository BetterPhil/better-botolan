# Join Health Programs: Nutrition, Dengue Control and TB Treatment

Public health programs you can join or ask about in Botolan.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- Families, children and anyone with health concerns covered by these programs.

## How it works

1. **Nutrition:** ask the RHU about child weighing, feeding and counseling.
2. **Dengue:** clean mosquito breeding places (the 4S strategy) and see a doctor early for fever.
3. **Tuberculosis:** a cough of two weeks or more? Go to the RHU. TB testing and treatment are free.
4. **Animal bites:** wash the wound with soap and water and go to a treatment center immediately.

## What to bring

- Valid ID
- PhilHealth number
- Child's health card
