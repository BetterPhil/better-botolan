# Get Check-ups, Basic Medicines and Vaccines

Where to get primary health care in Botolan.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- All residents. Every Filipino is automatically a PhilHealth member.

## How it works

1. **Go to your Rural Health Unit (RHU)** or barangay health station for check-ups, basic medicines and vaccines.
2. **Ask a Barangay Health Worker** about services and schedules.
3. **Bring your PhilHealth number** and ask about primary care (Konsulta).
4. **Follow referrals** to hospital care if needed.

## What to bring

- Any valid ID
- PhilHealth ID or number
- Your health record or booklet
