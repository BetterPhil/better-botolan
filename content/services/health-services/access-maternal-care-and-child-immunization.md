# Maternal Care and Child Immunization

Prenatal care, safe delivery and routine vaccines for children in Botolan.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- Pregnant women, new mothers and babies.
- Children who need routine vaccines.

## How it works

1. **Start prenatal checkups early** at the RHU or barangay health station.
2. **Plan a safe delivery** in a facility with a skilled birth attendant.
3. **Get newborn screening** and first vaccines.
4. **Complete routine childhood vaccines** (BCG, hepatitis B, polio, measles) on schedule.
5. **Keep the immunization card** and bring it every visit.

## What to bring

- Valid ID of the mother
- PhilHealth number
- Mother and Child Book
- Baby's birth certificate once available

- **Republic Act No. 9288** (Newborn Screening Act).
- DOH **Expanded Program on Immunization**.

## Tips

- Missed a vaccine? Go anyway; the worker can help you catch up.
