# Renew Your Business Permit and Pay Local Business Taxes

How and when to renew your Mayor's Permit and pay business taxes in Botolan.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- All existing businesses operating in Botolan, including each branch.

## How it works

1. **Renew during January.** Taxes and fees are generally due within the **first 20 days of January**.
2. **Quarterly option:** if allowed, pay on or before the **20th of January, April, July and October**.
3. **Declare last year's gross sales or receipts** on the BPLO form.
4. **Have your assessment computed** by the BPLO, then pay at the **Municipal Treasurer's Office**.
5. **Claim your renewed permit** and post it where customers can see it.

## What to bring

- Last year's Mayor's Permit and official receipts
- Sworn statement or audited FS of gross sales
- Current Barangay Clearance
- Sanitary Permit and FSIC if required
- Valid ID (and authorization if filing for another)

## Legal basis

- **Republic Act No. 7160**, Sec. 165: local taxes payable in the first 20 days of January or each quarter.
- Sec. 168: late payment can bring a **surcharge up to 25%** plus **interest up to 2% per month**.

## Tips

- Prepare papers in December to beat the January rush.
- Closing? File a **retirement** with the BPLO so taxes stop accruing.
