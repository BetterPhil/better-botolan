# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos or Tourism Promotions

How local producers and businesses can take part in fairs and promotions.

> **Please note:** This guide explains how this service generally works under Philippine law. Details specific to Botolan are marked **To be confirmed** until verified with the Municipal Government. Please call or visit the office before you go.

---

## Who it is for

- Local producers, food makers, craft makers and tourism businesses in Botolan.

## How it works

1. **Watch for announcements** from the Mayor's Office, municipal tourism/economic staff, and DTI Zambales.
2. **Get ready:** valid business permit, product samples, price list, short product profile.
3. **Check product rules.** Processed food and cosmetics usually need FDA registration.
4. **Apply or register** through the office that announced the event.
5. **Ask about a Negosyo Center** for free business advice and links to trade events.

## What to bring

- Valid business permit
- Product samples and price list
- DTI/SEC/CDA registration
- FDA registration where it applies
